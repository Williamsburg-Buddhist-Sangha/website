#### *Share a dish!*

Monday, November 2, at 5:30 pm.  
In the lobby, ‘Gathering Space’, of the Williamsburg Unitarian  
Join us for a delightful potluck dinner! Please bring your favorite vegetarian dish to share. No pressure, no business meeting, just good food and company!

Monday night’s meditation group will start at the usual time, 7 pm, after the potluck. Feel free to stay for the group if you want.  
Bring your own plates, cups and utensils if you can, but we will provide for those who need them.
