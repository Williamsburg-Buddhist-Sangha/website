Are you curious about Buddhism but not sure where to start?  
Join the Williamsburg Buddhist Sangha for our Introduction to Buddhism course exploring buddhist teachings and practice, a two-part introduction course for beginners.

**When:** Saturdays, November 7 & 21  
**Time:** 9:30Am-12:30pm  
**Where:** Williamsburg Unitarian Universalist Campus: Room 108

**Cost**: *Free to attend any donations are welcome*  
What we’ll explore:

- The Buddha’s life and the Four Noble Truths
- The Three Jewels and the roots of Buddhism in Asia
- Theravada and Mahayana teachings and how they’ve shaped Buddhism in America
- Mindfulness practices
- And More..

Expect teaching, group conversation, and guided practice. No experience needed.
