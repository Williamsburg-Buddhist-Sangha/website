*Daylong*

*With John Davis*

**Saturday morning, October 17, from 10:00 am to 4:00 pm**

On Zoom and in person at the Williamsburg Unitarian Universalist campus.

*You can join us for the entire time, or you join us as you are able. You can join at any time between 10:00 am and 3:00 pm. You can also leave at any time. We just ask that you come and go quietly.*

An opportunity to meditate a little longer than we usually do in our groups and at our events. This is an opportunity to just meditate, no frills, just sitting and walking.

***For experienced meditators***

There will be a brief introduction and instruction and then we will meditate, sitting or walking for about two hours, then lunch followed by more sitting and walking. **There will be ample opportunities for breaks.**

We will walk together, Zen style, with mostly a fairly normal walking pace. You can bow out of walking at any time. Chairs will be available for all; if you prefer to use a meditation cushion, please feel free to bring one.

**A light lunch will be provided.**

If you are planning on attending, please register so we will know how much lunch, etc., to prepare. Any donations will be appreciated, as they help offset the cost of lunch and a donation for the use of the space.
