The Williamsburg Buddhist Sangha is registered in the state of Virginia as a nonprofit corporation and is a tax-exempt public charity designated under section 501(c)3 of the US Internal Revenue Code. All contributions to the Williamsburg Buddhist Sangha are tax exempt to the fullest extent of the law.
